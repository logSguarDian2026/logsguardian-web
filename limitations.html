Limitations

Ten limitations, stated with their evidence

Each limitation below comes from the thesis's results and discussion chapters, or from its annexes. Where a result contradicts an earlier claim, we say so. The thesis is the reference, not the repository's internal notes.

01

The test set was read three times open

The held-out test set was read on 24, 25, and 26 September 2026 for the current model generation. The figures come from the last read. The thesis states that this does not fully meet its own reproducibility rule.

Thesis: Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados".

02

Metrics measure separation inside the corpus, not generalization open

Training, validation, and test partitions share the near-duplicate structure of the corpus. All 200 cmdi payloads of the SecLists set match a partition row under normalized equality, so that cmdi coverage is not evidence of generalization. A clean-corpus run is reported on the Evaluation page.

Thesis: Results, "Solapamiento del corpus de 590 payloads con el entrenamiento"; annex on near-duplicates.

03

Results depend on which sources are in training open

Excluding a single source changes detection a lot for some sources. Without modsec_learn, payloads_csv, or xss_dataset, the benign false positive rate rises to 76.0%, 27.0%, and 94.5%. The thesis did not evaluate the model with an alternative benign corpus.

Thesis: Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados"; annex on leave-one-source-out.

04

Browser User-Agents trigger false positives open

The User-Agent is one of the strongest features. Only 0.4% of benign training rows have a User-Agent longer than 40 characters, while 33.3% to 97.7% of attack rows do. With a browser User-Agent, the model blocked 97.1% of benign requests in OWASP Juice Shop (monitor mode) and 92.5% in dvws-node (block mode). The default Artillery User-Agent produced no false positives in the thesis's own app.

Thesis: SHAP results (ua_length, ua_present) and Results, "Falsos positivos en aplicaciones de terceros".

05

cmdi and sqli are confused in labels, not in blocking accepted tradeoff

In a re-measure with pair C, all 200 cmdi payloads of the corpus were labeled sqli. Of these, 198 were blocked and 2 were logged as pass_anomaly. The cause is that blind time-based techniques look alike across the two classes. The effect is on triage labels, not on the blocking decision.

Thesis: Results, Config 2 and the cmdi/sqli attribution note.

06

Part of the cmdi data is synthetic and not reproducible from the repository open

261 of the 1,468 cmdi test rows (17.8%) and 1,221 of the 6,853 cmdi training rows (17.8%) are synthetic. Their generator is not versioned in the repository, so those rows cannot be regenerated from the code.

Thesis: Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados".

07

The 0.35 threshold was calibrated on an earlier model open

The 0.35 blocking threshold was calibrated on validation for rf_v3 and was not recalibrated for rf_v11. The reported test metrics use argmax and do not apply the threshold, so no test metric exists for the deployed blocking rule.

Thesis: Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados" and Random Forest section.

08

Isolation Forest never blocks, and its contribution was measured on validation only by design

On validation, Isolation Forest flagged 19 of the 39 attacks that Random Forest let through. Blocking those flags would also block 866 benign requests, so the component only logs. Its contribution was not evaluated on the test set.

Thesis: Conclusions, objective 1–2; Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados".

09

Memory and CPU criteria are not met, and volume latency fails open

Added memory is +331.9 to +375.9 MiB, against a 150 MB criterion. CPU rises by +529.6% to +614.4% under normal and attack load, against a 15% criterion. Under volume load, p95 overhead reaches 6,495.46 ms and 7.00% of requests time out. The measured cost is for the whole container, so it cannot be attributed to one component. A monitoring run's leak indicator is inconclusive.

Thesis: Results, latency and memory/CPU sections; Conclusions, general objective.

10

Test features do not include the query rebuild effect open

The test set is vectorized from stored strings, but the middleware rebuilds the query with URLSearchParams before extracting features. For the same request this can change some feature values, for example quote_count from 1 to 0. The test metrics do not include this effect, which was not measured. A later commit stops re-encoding the query, but it postdates the thesis evaluations.

Thesis: Results, "Limitaciones de los resultados".

Also in scope

Other boundaries stated in the thesis

No online learning

Models are trained offline and shipped fixed with each release. Continuous learning was excluded from the project's scope, and the library does not retrain from your traffic.

Four attack classes only

logSguarDian covers SQL injection, XSS, path traversal, and command injection within a single request. DDoS and brute force are out of scope because they depend on several requests.

Data provenance gaps

The source of data restored on 23 September 2026 is not documented. The corpus has 383,202 rows in features, and 383,083 in unified.jsonl. The 119-row difference is the simulated telemetry source.

Round 5 is not a blind evaluation

The model was rebuilt after the Round 5 results were known, and the history does not link the two events. The 0.1.1 package runs in that round were executed by the co-author and are reported in the annex.
